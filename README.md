# Hi, I'm Wanderson 👋

### Websites & AI solutions that bring businesses more customers.

I design, build and launch fast, mobile-first websites and web apps for small and growing businesses — and add AI where it actually saves time or wins customers.

## What I do

- **Business websites** — clear, fast sites built to turn visitors into customers, with SEO basics done right.
- **Web apps** — custom tools that replace spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads, installable on the phone (PWA).
- **AI integrations** — chatbots, customer-service automation and content generation using AI APIs.
- **Launch & hosting** — domain, deploy, Docker/Nginx on a VPS, and ongoing maintenance.
